How the Kitty works ▾

Every matchday

Your team wins → every other player pays ₹500 into the pot.
Your team draws → every other player pays ₹250.
Your team loses → nothing changes. Once both your teams are knocked out, you stop paying in.

Winning the Kitty

Finishing “at the top” = your team comes 1st, 2nd, or reaches the semis while no other player's team is still standing. Top player takes 70% of the pot, second takes 30%.

Champion salami

If your team wins it all, every player chips in a ₹500 bonus salami.

The GF clause

Two players out at the same stage? Settled on Goals For — most goals scored across the tournament wins. Not the girlfriend clause.
